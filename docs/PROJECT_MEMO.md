# ME 2.0 — project memo
Updated: 2026-10-28 (America/New_York).
Current authority: the user's seed-first and semantic-first decisions. Research proposals remain hypotheses until tested.

## Why the project arrived here

Earlier ME explored explicit strategy sampling/enumeration, descriptors, realization and search. The user sought a stronger use of existing LLM reasoning and semantic knowledge without rebuilding its conceptual spine as a large hard-coded space.

The selected entry route therefore became seed-first: generate a small set of strategies directly against the brief, then develop a method that constructs a focused, expandable space from them. The space must contain possibilities beyond the observed seeds and support relevant cross-dimensional alternatives.

The latest change moves the proposed boundary primarily to semantic embedding space. The nine axes remain a consistent grounding and comparison framework. This is continuity with prior ME, not a reset.

## Five immediate tasks

Follow the [Step 02–03 plan](STEP02_03_SEMANTIC_FIRST_PLAN.md):
1. Consistent strategic decisions, semantic descriptions and nine-axis outputs.
2. Validate an existing embedding representation before considering training.
3. Link semantics and axes through explicit source-grounded strategy decisions.
4. Define and test membership and provisional expansion.
5. Provide minimal generation of unseen complete/partial candidates.

Do not assume a reversible embedding-to-axis map, nine disentangled embedding directions, arbitrary-vector decoding, useful coverage from blank fields, or creativity from embedding spread.
