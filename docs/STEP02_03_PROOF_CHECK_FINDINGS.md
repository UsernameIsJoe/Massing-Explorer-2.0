## Finding

Both A and B introduced commitments outside the coordinate-wise options observed in their five original seeds. Neither batch was merely a rearrangement of already-observed values. Every addition also has at least one newly represented state on a disclosed operational checklist. This supports strategically relevant scope-extension candidates, not proven usefulness, feasibility, or enlargement of an independently known architectural boundary.

The previous inference that the relevant-case strategy “did not work” was unsupported. A exceeded B on the earlier pair-token counts, but nearest-parent grouping distance is identical on average, and their numbers of new individual coordinate values are close. B was successful at targeted gap completion and also introduced unsolicited new options.

## 1. A measurable but limited boundary account

Let S be the original five seeds, and O_j(S) the known values they exhibit on coordinate j. Define an observed marginal envelope E(S) = product_j O_j(S). This is an outer combinatorial envelope, not a continuous hull or a learned manifold. A case is outside it when any known coordinate has a value absent from O_j(S). Only actual supplied cases are assessed; inconsistent synthetic combinations inside the product are not asserted to be viable.

Outside means the recorded options must expand to include that case. Inside means its individual values are already available, not that its joint arrangement is covered or semantically familiar. New options can also be poor designs. Unencoded architectural distinctions cannot be classified by this test.

Floor-set categories are retained literally. A separate numeric-range sensitivity asks whether any department occupies a level below or above the seed minimum/maximum. For example, arts floor 2 is new relative to {1} and {3}, but is not numerically outside the range 1-3.

## 5. Boundary sensitivity

As a secondary test, use the pooled 3-, 5- and 8-seed responses as the reference (16 records; 15 complete, with known values retained from the incomplete one). The actual experiment still used only the original five as parents.

| Reference envelope | A cases outside | B cases outside |
|---|---:|---:|
| Actual five-parent envelope | 5/5 | 5/5 |
| Pooled 16-initial-seed envelope | 3/5 | 2/5 |

Against the pooled initial envelope, A-E01/A-E03/A-E05 and B-E01/B-E03 still introduce marginal values. The remaining cases recombine values already represented somewhere in those initial responses. They can still introduce meaningful joint arrangements; inside this envelope does not mean useless or redundant. This sensitivity makes the reference-dependence explicit.

Interpreting A-E01’s floor 3 as a void and recording only floor 2 for gym/dining leaves the five-parent outside classifications and the A new-value counts (15/3/2) unchanged; the option identities for those two floor coordinates change. The raised-assembly operational difference remains. Its constructibility is not established.

## 6. Corrected conclusion

1. A and B both expand the explicitly observed strategic options beyond the five seeds; B did not merely fill internal gaps.
2. Both contain concrete, brief-relevant operational alternatives. This is stronger evidence than raw pair-token counts alone, but not a measured quality/usefulness advantage.
3. A’s pair-token lead does not demonstrate that its collection defines a better boundary. First-order option differences are modest and average nearest-parent grouping distance is identical.
4. Specific targeted gap completion, new-value introduction, new operational alternatives, feasible domain expansion and architectural quality are different outcomes.
5. A full architectural boundary cannot be recovered from the missing axes or from five selected samples without further assumptions. This analysis supplies an explicit, limited scope account; it does not retroactively validate the earlier experiment as a boundary test.
6. Neither code-only case expansion nor the overall relevant-case construction strategy was tested by the earlier A/B comparison. No failure of either is established.
