# ME 2.0 — current backbone
Updated: 2026-10-28 (America/New_York).
Status: revisable research direction; no production space constructor or validated embedding boundary.

## Purpose

Architectural strategies should remain meaningful, referenceable and explorable before expensive dimensional realization. Begin with a small set of brief-responsive LLM seeds and construct a focused but flexible space containing additional, uninstantiated possibilities.

Restricting later search to that space is intentional. The goal is useful contextual exploration, not exhaustive coverage or a global optimum. Relevant outside additions can extend the region; preserve their reasons and uncertainty.

Preserve prior ME/ME2.0 representations, benchmark, experiments, checking/realization experience and archive/refinement concepts. This is a method and supporting tools for established LLMs, not a new LLM or access to its hidden reasoning.

## Current development scope

Only Steps 02 and 03 are being specified now. The [semantic-first development plan](STEP02_03_SEMANTIC_FIRST_PLAN.md) is the working research document.

1. Comparable seed decisions, semantic descriptions and nine-axis records.
2. Validated embedding representation, starting with existing models.
3. Explicit many-to-many links between semantic intentions, decisions and axes.
4. Tested joint membership and provisional expansion rules.
5. Minimal generation of unseen complete or partial strategies.

The region is proposed to be primarily semantic; axes provide grounding, controls and cross-checks. A region is not merely an archive, plot, descriptor envelope or a collection of good-sounding labels. Embedding proximity is not quality or feasibility; membership rules do not themselves generate candidates.

## Settled directions

- Step 02 can proceed without an optimal seed count. Five existing parents preserve continuity; five is not mandatory.
- Keep the nine-axis framework consistent. Revisit evolution only upon a concrete failure.
- Partial strategies and explicit blanks are promising; their allowed scope and completion method remain unresolved. Missing information does not establish coverage.
- Provisionally admit an outside alternative with an explainable connection to the brief and meaningful strategic difference. Superiority and dimensional feasibility need not be proved first.
- Investigate fixed commitments plus flexible alternatives/ranges. Semantic versus implementation locks are proposed distinctions, not a selected mechanism.
- Retain conflicts, uncertainties and predicted consequences as such. Do not mislabel untested seeds as legal designs or model confidence as calibrated probability.
- Advanced-model access is manual chat. Prefer batched reasoning and code/local models where appropriate; record transfer/review cost.

## Broad workflow — later details deliberately open

Interpret the brief -> generate strategic seeds -> construct the semantic possibility space -> explore under iterative briefs/selections -> shortlist -> realize and test -> revise/focus/archive or re-search -> present the preferred result and trade-offs.

History and evidence accumulate throughout. User feedback or realization can reopen strategic exploration. Later search policies, weights, stopping rules, narrowing thresholds, repair methods and realization budgets are not fixed by this outline.

## Working principles

Prioritize intelligence and useful reasoning over automation volume. Compare added machinery against a simple brief/Markdown/history plus LLM baseline.

The user directs the conceptual spine. Explain the logic and mathematics; use code to test mechanisms rather than hiding them behind implementation.

Research neuroscience and ML independently of illustrative suggestions. Distinguish established findings, analogies and proposed architectural applications. Start with small, discriminating checks under real constraints.
