## Mandatory department assignment

Each of the nine departments must be assigned entirely to exactly one principal mass. Splitting any department across masses is prohibited, including custodial/maintenance and individual support rooms. A department may span floors only within its assigned mass, on contiguous occupied floors with at least 70 m² (approximately 753 SF) of scheduled department NFA on each occupied floor.

## Planning assumptions

- Dimensions in feet unless otherwise stated; areas in square feet.
- Historical workbook display: 66,405 SF. This is reference information, not the governing design target for this study.
- Governing GFA target: 44,270 × 1.15 × 1.50 = 76,365.75 SF, with ±3% tolerance (74,074.7775–78,656.7225 SF). Double-height voids are not additional occupied floor area.
- Default classroom configuration: double-loaded bar, 30-ft classroom depth and 8-ft corridor.
- Typical floor-to-floor height used for mass extrusion: 14 ft.
- Gym: clear dimensions at least 60 × 100 ft; double height.
- Cafeteria: clear dimensions at least 40 × 60 ft; the brief separately specifies that gym and dining are together and double height.
- Daylight planning: preferred maximum **width** 90 ft for departments with instructional/daylit rooms.
- If a footprint uses a residual L-shaped floor arm, minimum arm depth is 20 ft.
- If a department spans multiple floors, those floors must be contiguous, and each floor portion of that department must contain at least **70 m² (~753 SF)**.
- No specific site boundary, adjacent buildings, orientation, terrain, or access road geometry is supplied.

The brief specifies design requirements and preferences. If a project assumption and an explicit brief item conflict, prioritize the brief and explain material uncertainties. For this study the adjusted target governs; do not substitute the workbook display. The 60-m cap applies to the long principal dimension of each mass, not the combined building extent. The ratio applies to each mass's ground footprint, short side divided by long side, nominally 0.40–0.625. The shared validator uses an absolute ratio tolerance of 0.00675, disclosed equally for all systems. Academic/special-education width means the containing bar depth, not a separate room width.

# Generator case format v1.0

This is an abstract strategic record, not geometry. Use supplied MODEL_INPUT.md and ROOM_PROGRAM.csv as authority. Keep room names/quantities/areas unchanged; you need not repeat all rooms in each case.

Department codes:
D01 = CORE ACADEMIC
D02 = SPECIAL EDUCATION
D03 = ART & MUSIC
D04 = HEALTH & PHYSICAL EDUCATION
D05 = MEDIA CENTER
D06 = DINING & FOOD SERVICE
D07 = MEDICAL
D08 = ADMINISTRATION & GUIDANCE
D09 = CUSTODIAL & MAINTENANCE

Return one JSON object with:
- cases: array of requested cases.
- collection_differences: up to 120 words identifying consequential differences across the collection.

Each case:
- case_id: S01,S02,... for seeds; E01,E02,... for additions.
- name: short descriptive label.
- organizing_principle: 20–45 words of concise design rationale, not a detailed reasoning trace.
- masses: array of {mass_id, label}. IDs are local arbitrary labels such as M1; they carry no ordering, height or coordinates.
- departments: nine entries {department_id, mass_ids, occupied_floors}.
  mass_ids: complete list of assigned mass IDs, or null if genuinely unresolved.
  occupied_floors: complete union of proposed occupied levels (ground=1), or null if genuinely unresolved.
  A multi-floor department is not automatically a multi-mass department.
  If a department is proposed in more than one mass, retain the explicit list and explain the conflict; do not conceal it by calling each part a new department. The brief prohibits this assignment.
- connections: each unordered pair of defined masses, with {mass_a, mass_b, direct_link}.
  direct_link=true: explicitly propose a direct built connection, such as a bridge or connecting passage.
  false: explicitly no such built connection; shared grounds or outdoor walking alone do not count.
  null: connection genuinely unresolved. Do not invent site distances or orientation.
- main_tradeoff: 15–35 words, naming a consequential sacrifice or tension.
- unresolved_notes: up to four brief strings for unknowns, potential requirement conflicts or later dimensional checks; empty array permitted.

Use null for unresolved information, not an invented answer. Do not use null to avoid making all strategic decisions. Explain a genuinely unresolved commitment briefly. Retain any inconsistency visibly; do not assert that all requirements are verified.
A schema-valid JSON record is not a feasibility certificate. Detailed dimensions, geometry and preference scores are not requested.

The attached case.schema.json specifies syntax. Some conflicts are deliberately representable so they can be recorded and revised; its permissiveness does not waive the brief.
