Pilot 01 · Strategy triplets

Pick the option that is more like the same strategy as the anchor: the one you would call a variation of that scheme rather than a different idea. Weigh what is grouped together, how the masses relate, what is raised or stacked, and what the scheme is trying to achieve. Ignore wording, brief compliance and which design is better. If one option shares the structure and the other shares the intention, choose what you think defines the strategy and say why in the note. Use "About equal" only when you truly cannot separate them.
