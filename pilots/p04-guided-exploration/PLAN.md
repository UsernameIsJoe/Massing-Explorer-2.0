# Pilot 4: does embedding guidance improve LLM exploration?

Status: **draft for architect review**, 2026-10-29. Nothing has run. Format v3 approved by the architect; prompts **locked** 2026-10-29 (`data/PROMPT_LOCK.json`). The 8 chats wait for the architect's go. Agreement in force: no mass case generation while piloting.

## Question

Does steering the LLM with the embedding, together with the axes, produce more strategies that are new in meaning *and* new in structure than steering with the axes alone, or no steering at all? The comparison is at equal LLM budget, with validity and text–record agreement held at least equal.

This is the central untested claim ([CORE_CONTRIBUTION.md](../../docs/CORE_CONTRIBUTION.md); [project review](../../docs/PROJECT_REVIEW_2026-10-29.md) §4). It also pilots output format v3 ([OUTPUT_FORMAT_v3.md](OUTPUT_FORMAT_v3.md)).

## Design: 2 × 2, four arms

| | No axis guidance | Axis guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **No semantic guidance** | **U**: unguided; "propose strategies different from these" | **A**: axis gaps, described in words |
| **Semantic guidance** | **S**: meaning directions chosen with the embedding | **J**: the same axis gaps as A, plus semantic context chosen with the embedding |

- **J vs A** isolates what the embedding adds on top of the axes. This is the decisive comparison.
- **S vs U** shows what semantic steering does without axes.
- The 2 × 2 shows whether the two kinds of guidance add up or overlap.

**Everything else is held equal across arms:**
- The same brief and program.
- The same archive shown to the model: the 15 existing seeds' names and concepts. Every arm sees the same information about what exists; only the direction differs.
- The same output format v3, model, effort setting and number of cases.

## Steering view (architect, 2026-10-29)

Semantic guidance in S and J uses the **idea view** only (`concept` + `intent`). The move and unresolved views are embedded for every output and analysed as exploration. Steering by them is left for a later pilot, once it is known how much steering by the idea view does. See [EMBEDDING_VIEWS.md](EMBEDDING_VIEWS.md).

## Directions: code-generated and deterministic, locked before running

1. **Axis gaps (A, J).**
   - Coarse strategy cells are link pattern (8) × special education's mass (with academic / with commons / third mass) × height profile (all three-storey / mixed / mostly low).
   - The 15 seeds occupy 10 cells. The Pilot 3 generator shows 35 more cells that are unoccupied and contain valid structures.
   - 10 of those 35 are drawn, stratified by link pattern with a fixed random seed.
   - Each gap is described in plain words, for example "masses linked in a chain through the third mass; special education in the third mass; all masses three storeys". The prompt asks for a *meaningful idea* that leads there.
2. **Semantic directions (S).** From bge-m3 embeddings of the 15 archive concepts, three kinds:
   - **away:** the densest meaning cluster's concepts, with the instruction to propose an idea unlike all of them;
   - **bridge:** two concepts far apart in meaning, asking for an idea that combines the intent of one with the organization of the other;
   - **develop:** a concept from the thinnest cluster, asking for a different idea in its spirit.

   10 directions are drawn in fixed proportions: 4 away, 3 bridge, 3 develop.
3. **Joint context (J).** Each of A's 10 gaps, plus two semantic additions:
   - the 2 archive concepts nearest in meaning to the gap's nearest structures, with the instruction not to rephrase them;
   - 1 archive concept far in meaning, with the instruction that its intent may be borrowed.

## Budget and procedure

- **Size:** 4 arms × 2 chats × 5 cases = **40 cases, 8 chats**.
- **Model:** one model, the same one as before: ChatGPT, temporary chat, medium effort. Each chat is fresh. Confirmed by the architect, 2026-10-29.
- **Order:** the 8 chats are run in an order randomized in advance.
- **Locking:** prompts are generated by code from one template. Only the direction block differs. Each prompt is hashed (SHA-256) before running.
- **Replies:** saved raw. Cases get random IDs before scoring, so scoring is blind to arm.

## Measures

Two embedding models measure meaning: mxbai and nomic. The embedding that steers (bge-m3) is **never** used to judge the outcome. Otherwise the steering embedding would win by construction.

| Measure | Definition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **New strategy (primary)** | Valid, **and** new in meaning, **and** new in structure | Primary |
| ↳ new in meaning | Farther than the paraphrase threshold from every archive case and every earlier case of the same arm, under **both** measurement embeddings. The threshold is the 95th percentile of original-to-paraphrase distances from the 15 Pilot 01c rewrites | Part of primary |
| ↳ new in structure | Weighted change ≥ 1.0 from every archive case and earlier same-arm case, by the architect's decision weights (the architect's own bar for "a different strategy") | Part of primary |
| Validity | Passes all hard rules and reference checks | Guardrail |
| Text–record agreement | Extractor agreement per trait (format v3 validation step 6) | Guardrail; also checks format v3 |
| Steerability | A and J: did the case land in its target gap? S: did it move in the requested meaning direction (bge-m3, as a manipulation check only) | Diagnostic |
| Axis coverage | New coarse cells reached | Diagnostic; favours A and J by design, so never primary |
| Forced or real | Architect, blind, 12 cases (3 per arm) plus 2 repeated cases, rated 1–5 for "a real architectural idea" vs "forced". Agreed 2026-10-29 | **Veto only, weighted lightly** (see below) |

## How the blind check is used

The architect asked that the method not over-rely on this rating: a single reviewer can be tired, rushed or subjective. So the rating is used as follows:

- **It can only veto, never decide.** It never creates a "win".
- **A veto needs a clear signal.** The winning arm's mean must be at least 1.5 points below the comparison arm's, **and** the 2 repeated cases must be rated within 1 point of themselves. If the repeats disagree, the check is reported but not used.
- **The architect's free-text impressions** are recorded as qualitative notes, not scores.

## Decision rules, fixed before any reply

This is a pilot: 10 cases per arm can show a signal, not prove an effect.

| Outcome | Reading | Next |
|---|---|---|
| J beats A by ≥ 2 new strategies out of 10, and J's validity and agreement are within 1 case of A's | **Promising:** the embedding adds value | Scale up: more chats, a second model, then the grounding corpus in format v3 |
| J roughly equals A (within 1), and both beat U | The embedding adds nothing detectable on top of axes | Check S vs U. If S > U, semantic steering helps alone but overlaps with the axes, so redesign J's semantic context. If S ≈ U, the evidence leans toward the fallback (axis-gap exploration with semantic deduplication) |
| Guided arms lose ≥ 2 cases on validity or agreement | Guidance makes outputs forced | Fix the prompt before anything else |
| Format v3 traits still not carried (agreement on special education or vertical below the Experiment 1 ceiling) | Format v3 does not work yet | Revise format v3 before scaling |
