Pilot 3 · Expansion review

Each item is a new strategy that is one defining move away from one of your seeds, outside every seed's region. Decide whether it is worth adding to the possibility space: a brief-relevant strategy worth exploring, even if unproven. It does not have to be better than the seed or proven feasible. About 15 seconds each.

  1. A Add: a real strategy worth exploring
  2. M Maybe: interesting but doubtful
  3. S Skip: not a sensible strategy for this brief
